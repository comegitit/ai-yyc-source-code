How I Apply AI In Practice

Business problem first, technology second. Two systems this year: one for a major Alberta lender, one my own.

SIGNAL: AI-Assisted Credit Monitoring

Project Manager

A proof-of-concept decision-support system for commercial credit monitoring. SIGNAL turns document intake into rated, evidence-backed early warnings, with every credit decision left to a qualified human.

Reviewing one borrower's quarterly financials by hand takes an analyst 7 to 8 hours. The team modelled SIGNAL at under 3 hours, with review capacity up 50% to 60%.

I led delivery across a four-person team and helped set the boundaries the system works within: no credit approvals, no risk ratings, no live banking data.

Agentic AI Job-Search System

Designed & Built Solo

An autonomous agent that has run my own job search every morning since May 2026. It reads overnight job alerts, scores each posting against a structured career profile, and drafts tailored, application-ready documents for the strongest matches, producing roughly 30 high-quality application packages a month.

Nothing goes out without my approval, and the agent was built to stay inside each job board's terms of service even where that cost it capability. That pattern, autonomous intake with structured scoring and a human approving the output, is what makes AI agents defensible inside an enterprise.
