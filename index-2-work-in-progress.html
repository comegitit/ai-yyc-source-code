How I Apply AI In Practice

Business problem first, technology second. Two systems from this year: one delivered with a major Alberta-based financial institution, one I built and run myself.

SIGNAL: AI-Assisted Credit Monitoring

Project Manager

A proof-of-concept decision-support system built with a major Alberta-based financial institution to modernize commercial credit monitoring. Credit teams reviewing quarterly financials by hand catch warning signs late, which narrows the options available to lender and borrower alike. SIGNAL automates the path from document intake to rated, evidence-backed early warnings, while every credit decision stays with a qualified human.

I led delivery across a four-person team and defined the boundaries the system operates within: no credit approvals, no risk ratings, no live banking data.

Agentic AI Job-Search System

Designed & Built Solo

An autonomous agent that has run my own job search every morning since May 2026. It reads overnight job alerts, scores each posting against a structured career profile, and drafts tailored, application-ready documents for the strongest matches, producing roughly 30 high-quality application packages a month.

Nothing goes out without my approval, and the agent was built to stay inside each job board's terms of service even where that cost it capability. That pattern, autonomous intake with structured scoring and a human approving the output, is what makes AI agents defensible inside an enterprise.
